## Frequently Asked Questions About AION 2 Steam

### Will AION 2 Steam support full cross-play with mobile and standalone PC players?
Yes, the title is built with a unified multi-platform backend. Steam users share persistent servers with players utilizing both the native NCSoft PC client and authorized mobile platforms. Cross-play matchmaking is fully supported across instanced dungeons, ranked arena battlegrounds, and open-world guild sieges.

### Can I transfer my existing characters if I switch to AION 2 Steam?
Character progress is saved at the server account level. As long as you link your existing publisher profile to your Steam account upon initial startup, all previously unlocked characters, cosmetics, and inventory items will appear seamlessly when logging in through Steam.

### Are server queues separated for Steam players during high-traffic launch events?
Queue algorithms operate on a unified server-capacity model rather than segregating users by launcher type. However, Steam users authenticate through Valve's login routing, which can occasionally process token validation faster than external standalone login portals during massive traffic surges.

### How do seasonal server wipes and faction resets work on Steam?
Standard realms are permanent and do not wipe progression or character levels. In contrast, designated seasonal war realms reset territorial control, guild rankings, and competitive fortress privileges at the conclusion of three-month cycles. All primary gear templates and core character progression carry over into permanent legacy clusters.
