### Can I miss a hidden cube expansion permanently if I outlevel a zone?
No. In *Aion 2*, low-level exploration and Shugo quests remain accessible even if you enter high-level or endgame zones. You can return to lower-level territories at any time to complete prerequisite quests and unlock your missed storage rows.

### Are open-world hidden treasure cubes shared across the entire server?
